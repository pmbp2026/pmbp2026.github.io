The 2026 Workshop On

Precision Many Body Physics

June 10-12, 2026 | Kingston upon Thames, Greater London

Register Now

Scope

The workshop will focus on the theoretical and experimental control of correlated quantum matter, highlighting recent progress and future directions. It will bring together theorists and experimentalists who share the goal of understanding and quantitatively predicting equilibrium properties and dynamical phenomena in strongly correlated many-body systems. Achieving this goal requires cross-validation between computational, experimental, and analytical approaches across a range of fields, including ultracold atoms, materials science, quantum chemistry, and nuclear physics.
